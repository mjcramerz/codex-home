# Form intake checklist

Consult this reference when form intake checklist is relevant to the selected task. Extract the specific constraint or example you need, verify version-sensitive behavior against the active toolchain, and return to the task rather than loading unrelated references.

Collect before opening Acrobat:
- source form filename and revision date
- required signer names, roles, and contact points
- mandatory fields, dates, IDs, or attachments
- output filename and submission target

Flag early:
- fields the user cannot answer yet
- signatures that require another person
- forms that need flattening or printing before submission
