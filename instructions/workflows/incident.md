# Contain and recover
Stop the affected MCP target or connection without deleting evidence or unrelated containers. Revoke and rotate exposed credentials through the owning service. Avoid collecting raw tool traffic or complete container inspect output into reports.

Determine which identity, mounts, network paths and credentials were reachable. Preserve minimal access-controlled evidence and note timestamps. Restore verified code and state, re-run the relevant negative and functional tests, and document the cause, impact, remediation and remaining uncertainty. Do not restart a compromised configuration merely to clear an alert.
